      Guide

      The Games Desk

      1. Open a room and send your friend its code, or the link.
      2. Suggest a game once you are both in. Your friend accepts or declines; whoever suggests a game moves first.
      3. Play. The server throws the dice and deals the cards, so neither of you sees the other's hand.
      4. Save to a file from the table at any time, and bring it back here to carry on.

      The game is kept on the Archives' server. Nothing about you is sent: only the room's code and your moves.
